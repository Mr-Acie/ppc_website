## BRAND VOICE CHEAT-SHEET

1. **Defiant + rallying.** The movement exists because a wrong has been done. Name it. Seniors were written off by the tech industry, and that's a provable lie worth pushing back on. Never whine — declare.
2. **Warm + dignified.** The audience has lived through things most people can't imagine. Write to their intelligence and their experience. They are not "the elderly." They are Mighty Elders.
3. **Plain English only.** No jargon. If you must use a tech word ("ChatGPT," "AI," "phishing"), define it in the same sentence. Read it back: would your grandmother understand this on the first pass?
4. **Never patronizing.** Words and phrases to eliminate: "even seniors," "surprisingly," "simple for you," "don't worry," "you can do it too." Treat the audience as capable adults who have been under-served, not as beginners who need hand-holding.
5. **Recurring refrain.** "They thought you were done. They were wrong." Use it as a rally cry — a chapter opener, a closer, a caption punchline. It carries the whole brand in six words.
